Advanced Chart — Live Trading & Analysis

LIVE CLICK TRADING from the chart: plan LONG and SHORT entries visually, set bracket-order entry, stop-loss and take-profit levels, and work with live broker-connected pricing.

  • Long / short chart trade plans and bracket order staging
  • Entry, stop and profit-target visualization
  • Adjust protective stops on the chart while managing an open position (where supported by the installed build and broker).
  • Live IBKR market data (subscription and broker connection required)
  • Fibonacci retracement and extension tools
  • Wyckoff analysis and harmonic XABCD pattern drawings
  • Trendlines, annotations and editable drawings
  • RSI, MACD, VWAP, moving averages and Bollinger Bands
  • Multi-timeframe analysis, zoom, pan and pop-out charts
  • Private local drawings and personal chart workspace

Features, live order routing and broker support vary by installed beta build and edition. Chart-based trading is not the same as an autonomous trading bot. Confirm the order details and paper-test before live use.
